Things I've
Built and Created

My work brings together technology, entrepreneurship, design, sustainability, storytelling, and social impact. Each project has given me an opportunity to explore a different problem, develop new skills, and turn an idea into something more concrete.

Aryavart

Aryavart is a digital platform dedicated to preserving Indian heritage through multi-format storytelling. The platform has grown organically to more than 5,000 readers, and working on it has allowed me to explore product development, brand strategy, community engagement, and creative storytelling. I'm kinda working on revamping it right now.

Reveroop

Reveroop is a community reuse platform developed as part of a sustainability fellowship (beVisioneers). The project focuses on reducing throwaway culture by encouraging people to reuse resources, and I worked on research and the development of an initial MVP.

Khaana Clock

Khaana Clock is a behavior-change concept designed to address household food waste. The prototype was tested with 7 families, giving me an opportunity to explore how research, design, and technology can be combined to encourage more responsible everyday choices. Also, IIT D's director himself appreciated it!

Tinker Champs

Through Tinker Champs, I have worked on web infrastructure, learning resources, handbooks, guide videos, and digital initiatives that support student innovation and entrepreneurship. This experience has allowed me to combine technology, communication, design, and education while contributing to initiatives that have reached students across the country. I can go on and on about this, but I'll just stop here :)
